Facing a DUI/DWI is stressful, and the right attorney makes a real difference. This interactive guide covers when to hire one, what the process looks like, and how to choose — with a checklist to keep you on track.

Educational only — not legal advice.

1

Know when to act

Time is critical. After a DUI arrest, you often have a short window (commonly 7-15 days) to request a hearing to protect your license. Don't wait — even an initial consultation helps you understand the clock you're on.

2

Understand the process ahead

A typical DUI case moves through stages: arraignment, pretrial motions, possible plea negotiations, and trial if needed. A good attorney explains where you are and what comes next so you are never in the dark.

3

Choose the right attorney

Prioritize DUI specialization, local court experience, transparent fees, and communication style. Meet more than one if you can, and pick the person you trust to represent you under pressure.

4

Set yourself up to move forward

Follow your attorney's advice, stay on top of court dates, and complete any required programs. And whatever the outcome, commit to never being in this position again — a planned sober ride is always cheaper than an attorney.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a drunk driving attorney and lawyer?

Nothing — "attorney" and "lawyer" are used interchangeably for the same role.

How do I find a good drunk driving attorney near me?

Look for DUI specialists in your county, check bar standing and reviews, and use free consultations to compare.

What should I bring to my first meeting?

Your citation, arrest paperwork, any test results, and a written timeline of what happened.
