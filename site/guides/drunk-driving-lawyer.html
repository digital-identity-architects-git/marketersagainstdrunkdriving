A good drunk driving lawyer can change the trajectory of your case. This interactive guide explains what they do, when you need one, and how to vet candidates — with a checklist you can tick off as you go.

Educational only — not legal advice.

1

Understand what a drunk driving lawyer does

A DUI/DWI lawyer protects your rights at every stage:

  • Reviews the traffic stop and arrest for procedural errors.
  • Challenges breath and blood test reliability.
  • Represents you at DMV/license hearings and in court.
  • Negotiates with prosecutors to reduce or dismiss charges.
2

Decide whether you need one

Strongly consider a lawyer if any apply:

  • It is a repeat offense.
  • Anyone was injured.
  • Your BAC was very high or testing is in dispute.
  • You could lose a license you need for work.

For a first, low-level offense some people self-represent — but the stakes are rarely worth the risk.

3

Vet your candidates

Use the same rigor you would for any major hire:

  • DUI-specific experience in your county.
  • Clear, transparent fees.
  • Good standing with the state bar.
  • Responsive communication.
4

Prepare for your first meeting

Bring everything you have: the citation, arrest paperwork, any test results, and a written timeline. The more organized you are, the more useful the consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a drunk driving lawyer cost?

It varies widely by region and complexity — often a few thousand dollars for a straightforward case and more if it goes to trial.

Can a lawyer get my DUI dismissed?

Sometimes, if there were procedural errors or weak evidence. No honest lawyer guarantees a dismissal.

Is it worth getting a lawyer for a first DUI?

Often yes — even a first offense carries lasting consequences a lawyer may help reduce.
