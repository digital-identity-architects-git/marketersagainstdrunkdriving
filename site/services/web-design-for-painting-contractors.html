Questions Painting Contractors ask us

How do we justify our price?

Prep. Publishing your actual preparation process — patching, caulking, priming, number of coats — makes the difference between your bid and the cheap one concrete. Homeowners cannot see prep in a quote, so you have to show it.

What is the best niche?

Cabinet refinishing. High margin, real search volume, and far fewer competitors than general painting.

How do we fill winter?

Interior and cabinet content, published in the autumn so it ranks before the season turns.
