What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every web design decision for plastic surgeons.

Extremely expensive paid competition
Medical tourism undercutting on price
Review and before-after content is heavily constrained
The objection we hear most: "Patients are pricing the same procedure in Mexico." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How patients researching elective cosmetic surgery search

Procedure and outcome specific: "tummy tuck recovery time", "breast augmentation cost [city]", "best plastic surgeon near me".

Questions Plastic Surgeons ask us

How do we compete with medical tourism?

By owning the complication and revision conversation. Honest content about what happens when an overseas procedure goes wrong, and about follow-up care, ranks well and reframes the price gap without disparaging anyone.

Recovery content or procedure content?

Recovery. It has more volume, less competition, and it is what patients are actually anxious about in the months before they book.

How important are galleries?

Decisive, and they must be consented, unedited and organized by procedure to be credible.
