What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every SEO decision for greenhouses & nurseries.

Big-box garden centres dominate the spring retail rush
Living inventory dies if it does not sell on schedule
Landscaper accounts are relationship-locked and hard to win
The objection we hear most: "People just go to the big-box store in April." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How homeowners, landscapers and municipal buyers search

Plant and problem specific: "native plants near me", "when to plant [crop] in [state]", "wholesale nursery [region]".

Who buys
the owner of a growing operation or garden centre
Decision cycle
Same day retail; seasonal contracts wholesale.
Deal value
Small retail transactions; substantial landscape and municipal contracts.
What proves you
Inventory that is actually current, growing expertise, and regional planting knowledge.
Seasonality
Spring is most of the year, with a secondary autumn window.

What SEO includes

Scoped for greenhouses & nurseries, not lifted from a generic retainer.

  • Keyword and intent map built around how buyers in your vertical actually search
  • Technical audit and fixes — crawl, speed, indexation, schema
  • Service and location page architecture that supports the whole practice
  • Internal linking that concentrates authority on the pages that earn revenue
  • Monthly reporting tied to leads, not vanity rank charts

Questions Greenhouses & Nurseries ask us

How do we compete with big-box garden centres?

On knowledge and on plants they do not carry. Regional planting guidance, native species and problem-solving content ranks well and attracts the gardener who has already been disappointed by a big-box purchase.

What is the best content investment?

A regional planting calendar. It is searched constantly, it ranks for years, and it establishes you as the local authority rather than a shelf.

How do we grow wholesale accounts?

Content written for landscapers — availability lists, sizing, contract growing. A completely different audience from retail and far less competitive.
