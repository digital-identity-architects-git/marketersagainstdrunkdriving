Questions Immigration Lawyers ask us

Can you do this in Spanish, Portuguese or Vietnamese?

Yes, and it should be real translation with native review, not machine output with hreflang tags. Half-translated immigration content reads as untrustworthy to exactly the audience you need.

Policy keeps changing. How do you keep up?

Content that touches processing times, fees or eligibility gets a scheduled review cycle. Anything volatile carries a visible last-reviewed date, which also helps with credibility.

How do we separate ourselves from notarios?

By making licensure and process legible on every page. It is a trust problem before it is a ranking problem.
