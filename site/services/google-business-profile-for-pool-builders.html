Questions Pool Builders & Service Companies ask us

Rates killed our volume. What now?

Shift the mix toward renovation and service. Resurfacing, equipment upgrades and recurring maintenance hold up when new construction does not, and the content for them is far less competitive.

When should we publish for summer?

December and January. Buyers research in winter and sign in spring — content published in April is a season late.

Fiberglass or gunite content?

The comparison page, first. It is where the decision actually happens.
