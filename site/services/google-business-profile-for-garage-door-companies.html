Questions Garage Door Companies ask us

How do we build trust in a scam-heavy category?

Publish prices. In a trade where every competitor says "call for pricing" and then triples the quote on arrival, a published spring replacement price is an enormous differentiator and it ranks for exactly what people search.

Repair or replacement focus?

Repair for volume and trust, replacement for margin. Repair customers become replacement customers within a few years if you handle them well.

Does curb appeal content work?

Yes, for replacement. Door style and material content catches the planning-stage buyer.
