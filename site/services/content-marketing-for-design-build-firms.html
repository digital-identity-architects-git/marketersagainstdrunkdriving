What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every content marketing decision for design-build firms.

Explaining the delivery model to owners who have never used it
Competing against both architects and general contractors at once
Search volume for the model itself is thin
The objection we hear most: "Owners do not know what design-build means." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How owners who want a single point of accountability search

Model-comparison driven: "design build vs design bid build", "design build contractor [city]", "benefits of design build".

the principal of an integrated design and construction firm

Questions Design-Build Firms ask us

How do we market a model nobody understands?

By owning the comparison. "Design build vs design bid build" is the single highest-leverage page in this business — it is where an owner decides on delivery method, and whoever explains it best usually wins the project.

Do we market as architects or contractors?

As the resolution to the conflict between them. That framing is the whole value proposition and it should lead every page.

Where does the volume come from?

From the underlying project-type searches, not the model name. We build both layers.
