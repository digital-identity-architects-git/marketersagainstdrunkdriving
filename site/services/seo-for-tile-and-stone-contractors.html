SEO for Tile & Stone Contractors

Rank for the searches homeowners remodeling bathrooms and kitchens actually run before they call anyone.

SEO for tile & stone contractors is a different job than SEO for anyone else, because homeowners remodeling bathrooms and kitchens do not behave like a general audience.

What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every SEO decision for tile & stone contractors.

Competing with general remodelers who subcontract tile out
Waterproofing failures from cheap competitors create category distrust
Highly craft-dependent work sold on price by everyone else
The objection we hear most: "Everyone bids this like it is commodity labor." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How homeowners remodeling bathrooms and kitchens search

Project-shaped: "walk in shower tile installation cost", "large format tile installer near me", "shower waterproofing".

Who buys
the owner of a tile installation company
Decision cycle
Weeks, usually as part of a larger remodel decision.
Deal value
Three to twenty thousand depending on scope.
What proves you
Detail photography, waterproofing system certifications, and craft-level explanations.

What SEO includes

Scoped for tile & stone contractors, not lifted from a generic retainer.

  • Keyword and intent map built around how buyers in your vertical actually search
  • Technical audit and fixes — crawl, speed, indexation, schema
  • Service and location page architecture that supports the whole practice
  • Internal linking that concentrates authority on the pages that earn revenue
  • Monthly reporting tied to leads, not vanity rank charts

Questions Tile & Stone Contractors ask us

How do we get paid for craft?

By making the invisible visible. Content on waterproofing systems, substrate prep and why showers fail gives homeowners a reason to care about who installs, and it is what people search after a leak.

Should we target remodelers or homeowners?

Both, on separate pages. Trade referral content and homeowner content need different language entirely.

What is the best-converting page?

Usually the shower remodel cost page. High intent, high value, and it maps directly to how people search.
