What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every SEO decision for real estate agents & teams.

Portals intercept nearly all property search traffic
Commission structures are under active pressure
Every agent in the market publishes identical content
The objection we hear most: "Zillow already won the search game." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How buyers and sellers in a specific market search

Neighborhood and process shaped: "[neighborhood] homes for sale", "is it a good time to sell in [city]", "how much is my house worth".

a team lead or individual agent

What proves you
Hyper-local market knowledge, transaction history, and neighbourhood-level detail portals cannot match.

What SEO includes

Scoped for real estate agents & teams, not lifted from a generic retainer.

  • Keyword and intent map built around how buyers in your vertical actually search
  • Technical audit and fixes — crawl, speed, indexation, schema
  • Service and location page architecture that supports the whole practice
  • Internal linking that concentrates authority on the pages that earn revenue
  • Monthly reporting tied to leads, not vanity rank charts

Questions Real Estate Agents & Teams ask us

Can we compete with the portals?

Not on listings, and you should stop trying. Neighbourhood-level content — schools, commute, HOA specifics, what actually sells there and why — is something a national portal cannot produce and local buyers search constantly.

What converts best?

Seller-side content. Home valuation and timing questions attract sellers, and listings are the asset in this business.

How do we handle commission pressure?

By publishing what you do. In a market where the service is invisible, describing it specifically is a defence of the fee.
