Questions Social Security Disability Lawyers ask us

How do we win with capped fees?

Condition-level content. "Disability for fibromyalgia" and its two hundred siblings are low-competition, high-intent, and cheap to rank for compared with the head term. That long tail is the entire game in this practice area.

How do we compete with non-attorney reps?

On the hearing. Make the ALJ stage concrete and the value difference becomes self-evident.

What about reaching clients who are unwell?

Text-first intake, not phone-first. It measurably raises contact rates in this vertical.
