Questions Mass Tort & Class Action Lawyers ask us

Can content move fast enough for a mass tort window?

Only if the infrastructure exists before the docket does. Firms that keep a standing template, a schema pattern and a publishing pipeline can be live in days. Firms that start from scratch are three months late. We build the pipeline first.

Owned content or purchased leads?

Both, but owned content is the only asset that survives the campaign. Purchased leads end when you stop paying.

What about the science?

It gets cited and reviewed. Mass tort content that overstates causation is a liability, not an asset.
