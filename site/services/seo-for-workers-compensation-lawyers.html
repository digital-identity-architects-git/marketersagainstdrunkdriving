What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every SEO decision for workers compensation lawyers.

Fee caps mean the economics only work at volume
Heavy competition from firms treating comp as a personal-injury add-on
Injured workers often speak English as a second language
The objection we hear most: "Fee caps make marketing hard to justify." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How injured workers whose claim was denied or underpaid search

Triggered by a document: "workers comp claim denied", "how long can I stay on workers comp", "can I be fired while on workers comp".

Who buys
a claimant-side workers comp firm
Decision cycle
Days to weeks, triggered by a denial letter or a benefits cutoff.
Deal value
Statutory fee caps in most states, so volume matters more than case size.
What proves you
Board and hearing experience, bilingual capability, and content that explains the process step by step.

Questions Workers Compensation Lawyers ask us

How does the math work under fee caps?

It works on cost per signed case, which is why we build around the denial and retaliation searches — high intent, lower competition than the head term, and they convert at a rate that survives the cap.

Do we need Spanish content?

In most industrial markets, yes, and it is usually the single biggest untapped channel in this practice area.

Is comp worth separating from personal injury?

Yes. Shared sites confuse both audiences and dilute the topical signal for each.
