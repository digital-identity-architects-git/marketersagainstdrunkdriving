What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every content marketing decision for orthodontists.

Direct-to-consumer aligner brands reset price expectations
General dentists now offer aligners and take the easy cases
Referral relationships are shifting under the practice
The objection we hear most: "Mail-order aligners quote a third of our fee." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How parents of teens and adults considering clear aligners search

Comparison and cost driven: "invisalign vs braces cost", "how much are braces for kids", "orthodontist near me free consultation".

Questions Orthodontists ask us

How do we answer the mail-order price?

With what goes wrong. Content on relapse, bite problems and cases that aligners cannot treat is accurate, searched heavily, and reframes your fee as supervision rather than product markup.

Should we target adults or parents?

Both, separately. Adult aligner searchers and parents of teens use completely different language and respond to different proof.

How do we protect referrals?

Publish for the referring dentist — complexity thresholds, when to refer. It strengthens the relationship rather than competing with it.
