Questions Lash & Brow Studios ask us

How do we justify our price against home artists?

Licensing and sanitation, made explicit. Most clients do not know that permanent makeup is regulated tattooing in their state. Publishing that fact and your credentials reframes the price gap as a safety gap.

Why healed results rather than fresh photos?

Because fresh microblading always looks good and healed work does not. Publishing healed results signals confidence and it is what informed clients search for.

How do we improve retention?

Automated fill reminders on the client actual cycle. It is unglamorous and it is the highest-return thing in the vertical.
