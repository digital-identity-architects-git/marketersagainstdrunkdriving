Questions Physical Therapy Clinics ask us

How do we get patients without referrals?

Direct access. Most states allow patients to see a PT without a physician referral and most patients have no idea. A single clear page explaining direct access in your state is often the highest-converting asset in the clinic.

How do we reduce dropout?

Automated between-visit content on home exercise and progress expectations. Completion rates rise and so does reimbursement per episode.

Should we build cash-pay services?

Yes — dry needling, sports performance and wellness programs escape reimbursement pressure entirely and are very searchable.
