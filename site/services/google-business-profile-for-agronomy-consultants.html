What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every google business profile decision for agronomists & crop consultants.

Producers see agronomy as free advice bundled with input purchases
Independence from input sellers is the value and it is hard to communicate
Small local market with intensely personal relationships
The objection we hear most: "The co-op gives agronomy advice for free with the fertilizer." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

Questions Agronomists & Crop Consultants ask us

How do we compete with free advice from input sellers?

By making independence concrete. Content about conflicts of interest in bundled agronomy, and about recommendations that reduced input spend, demonstrates the value in a way a sales pitch cannot. Producers already suspect it — you just have to say it.

Is the search volume there?

Low volume, very high value. A handful of contracts is a business, and the pest and disease queries producers run in-season are genuinely unserved.

What content works best?

In-season problem diagnosis. Publish it before the season and it earns the call when the problem appears.
