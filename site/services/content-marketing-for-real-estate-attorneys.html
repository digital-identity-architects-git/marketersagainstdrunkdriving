What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every content marketing decision for real estate attorneys.

Closing work is referral-locked through agents and lenders
Volume rises and falls with interest rates, entirely outside your control
Title companies compete for the same transactional work
The objection we hear most: "Agents send us the work. Search will not change that." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How buyers, sellers, investors and developers search

Transaction-triggered: "do I need a lawyer to buy a house in [state]", "how to evict a tenant", "what is a title defect".

Who buys
a partner at a real estate practice
Decision cycle
Days for closings, months for development matters.
Deal value
Flat fees for closings; substantial hourly work on development and litigation.
What proves you
Turnaround speed, agent relationships, and fee transparency.

What Content Marketing includes

Scoped for real estate attorneys, not lifted from a generic retainer.

  • Topical map covering the full decision journey in your vertical
  • Editorial calendar with a publishing cadence you can actually sustain
  • Long-form pieces written by someone who understands the subject matter
  • Internal linking and content refresh cycles so old work keeps earning
  • Conversion paths built into the content, not bolted on after

Questions Real Estate Attorneys ask us

Our referrals come from agents. Why publish?

Because the content that makes agents look smart to their clients is the content that keeps agents referring. Publishing for the referral source is a different strategy than publishing for the buyer, and it is usually the higher-return one here.

How do we survive a rate-driven slowdown?

By shifting the content mix toward landlord-tenant, title disputes and investor work, which run counter to the purchase cycle.

Is zoning work worth targeting?

Very. Low volume, high value, and almost nobody is publishing seriously about it.
