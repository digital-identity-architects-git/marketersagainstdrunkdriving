What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every local SEO decision for civil litigation attorneys.

Low search volume relative to the value of a single matter
Prospects often arrive having already been sued and panicking
Difficult to differentiate from every other litigation firm in town
The objection we hear most: "Every litigation firm site says the same three things." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How businesses and individuals in commercial or contract disputes search

Situation-driven: "I was served with a lawsuit", "breach of contract damages [state]", "how much does litigation cost".

Who buys
a litigation partner
Decision cycle
Weeks, triggered by a lawsuit being filed or threatened.
Deal value
Hourly, with substantial engagements on complex matters.
What proves you
Trial experience stated concretely, industry specialization, and honest cost framing.

Questions Civil Litigation Attorneys ask us

How do we differentiate?

By industry, not by adjective. "Construction defect litigation" or "partnership dissolution" are searchable and specific; "aggressive advocacy" is neither.

Volume is tiny. Is it worth it?

One matter often pays for a year of the program. We build for conversion quality and report on matters opened.

What converts best?

Content for the person who was just served. Highest urgency, clearest need, least competition.
