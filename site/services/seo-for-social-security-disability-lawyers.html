What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every SEO decision for social security disability lawyers.

Federal fee caps compress margins to nothing without volume
Non-attorney representatives compete for identical searches
Claimants are often in poor health and hard to reach by phone
The objection we hear most: "Capped fees mean we cannot outspend anyone." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How people denied SSDI or SSI benefits search

Condition-specific and denial-triggered: "SSDI denied what next", "can I get disability for [condition]", "how long is the disability hearing wait".

a disability practice, often high-volume

Federally capped contingency fees, so the model is volume-driven.

Approval rates, hearing experience, and content organized by medical condition.

Questions Social Security Disability Lawyers ask us

How do we win with capped fees?

Condition-level content. "Disability for fibromyalgia" and its two hundred siblings are low-competition, high-intent, and cheap to rank for compared with the head term. That long tail is the entire game in this practice area.

How do we compete with non-attorney reps?

On the hearing. Make the ALJ stage concrete and the value difference becomes self-evident.

What about reaching clients who are unwell?

Text-first intake, not phone-first. It measurably raises contact rates in this vertical.
