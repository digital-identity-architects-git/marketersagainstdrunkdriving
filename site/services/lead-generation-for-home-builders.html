What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every lead generation decision for custom home builders.

Extraordinarily long sales cycles with heavy tire-kicking
Cost-per-square-foot questions dominate every first conversation
Production builders dominate paid search with far bigger budgets
The objection we hear most: "Everybody asks about price per square foot and then disappears." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How families building a custom home on their own lot search

Cost and process shaped: "cost to build a house in [state]", "custom home builder near me", "how long does it take to build a house".

Questions Custom Home Builders ask us

How do we stop losing people at the price question?

By answering it first, in public, with ranges and what drives them. Builders who publish honest cost-per-square-foot content get fewer inquiries and dramatically better ones, because the unqualified filter themselves out before your sales time is spent.

The cycle is eighteen months. How do we stay in it?

A long-form email sequence covering lot selection, financing, allowances and change orders. It is the only channel that works at that duration, and almost no builder runs one.

Can we outrank production builders?

Not on the head terms and you should not try. Custom-specific and process-specific queries are where the qualified buyers are anyway.
