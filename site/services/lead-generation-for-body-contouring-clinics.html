Questions Body Contouring Clinics ask us

How do we manage the expectation problem?

By putting the twelve-week timeline in the marketing rather than the consent form. Practices that publish a realistic week-by-week timeline see fewer refund requests and better reviews — the content does the expectation-setting before money changes hands.

How has GLP-1 changed this?

Substantially. The new high-intent search is skin laxity and muscle loss after rapid weight loss. That content barely exists yet and it is the biggest open opportunity in the vertical.

Should we publish candidacy criteria that disqualify people?

Yes. It costs a few consultations and saves you the refunds and one-star reviews those consultations would have become.
