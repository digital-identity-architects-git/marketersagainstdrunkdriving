Questions Therapists & Mental Health Practices ask us

Can we outrank the directories?

Not for head terms, and you do not need to. Specialization plus insurance plus location — "EMDR therapist taking [insurance] in [city]" — is where practices win, and directories serve those queries badly.

We cannot use testimonials. What builds trust?

Writing. Genuinely useful content about the condition and about what therapy actually looks like does the work testimonials would. It is also the most ethical form of marketing in this field.

Is group practice different from solo?

Yes — group practices should build clinician pages plus specialty pages, so both searches land somewhere real.
