What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every SEO decision for business & corporate attorneys.

Search volume is low and the good clients arrive through networks
Competing against big-firm brand names for mid-market work
Hard to demonstrate expertise without disclosing client matters
The objection we hear most: "Our clients come from the network. Search is not our channel." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How founders, owners and in-house counsel at small and mid-sized companies search

Specific and unemotional: "LLC vs S corp [state]", "how to structure an asset purchase", "what to include in an operating agreement".

Who buys
a partner at a business-law firm
Decision cycle
Months. Relationship-driven, and usually triggered by a deal or a dispute.
Deal value
Hourly, with the real value in a multi-year general-counsel relationship.
What proves you
Deal experience stated in categories, industry specialization, and writing that demonstrates judgment rather than listing services.

What SEO includes

Scoped for business & corporate attorneys, not lifted from a generic retainer.

  • Keyword and intent map built around how buyers in your vertical actually search
  • Technical audit and fixes — crawl, speed, indexation, schema
  • Service and location page architecture that supports the whole practice
  • Internal linking that concentrates authority on the pages that earn revenue
  • Monthly reporting tied to leads, not vanity rank charts

Questions Business & Corporate Attorneys ask us

Is search worth it when volume is this low?

The volume is low and the value per conversion is enormous. Twelve of the right visitors a month can be a better year than a thousand of the wrong ones. We build for that math and report accordingly.

How do we show expertise without breaching confidence?

Write about the pattern, not the client. Structural analysis of a deal type demonstrates judgment far better than a logo wall, and it ranks.

Does LinkedIn matter more than the website here?

They work together. The partner posts, the site converts. We usually run both.
