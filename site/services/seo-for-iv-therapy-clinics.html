Questions IV Therapy & Wellness Clinics ask us

How do we market this without overclaiming?

By describing ingredients, protocol and who it suits rather than promising outcomes. It is more defensible and, in practice, it converts better than the breathless claims competitors make.

Where is the reliable demand?

Events, travel and athletic recovery. Building content around those occasions smooths a very lumpy demand curve.

Is mobile service worth marketing separately?

Yes, it is a different search and a much higher ticket.
