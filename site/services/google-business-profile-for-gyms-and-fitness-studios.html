Questions Gyms & Fitness Studios ask us

How do we fix the January-to-March collapse?

With onboarding, not acquisition. Automated email and SMS through the first six weeks, plus a structured beginner path, is what converts a resolution into a habit. Most gyms spend everything on getting people in and nothing on keeping them.

How do we compete with budget chains?

On coaching and community, which they structurally cannot provide. Content about beginner anxiety and personal attention reaches people the chains lose.

When do we publish for January?

October. The rankings have to exist before the demand arrives.
