What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every web design decision for laser & skin clinics.

Skin-of-color safety concerns that most marketing ignores entirely

Questions Laser & Skin Clinics ask us

Patients do not know device names. How do we get found?

You stop marketing the device and start marketing the condition. Melasma, rosacea, acne scarring and ingrown hairs are what people search. The device becomes the answer inside the page, not the headline on it.

Should we address skin-of-color safety?

Yes, and it is a genuine competitive opening. Most clinics say nothing, patients with darker skin are searching specifically, and being the clinic that answers wins that entire segment.

How do we fill the winter?

Winter is the season. We front-load resurfacing content in September so the booking curve is full by November.
