What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every email marketing decision for solar installers.

Predatory door-to-door sales has poisoned public trust
Incentive and net-metering rules change constantly
High-pressure national competitors with aggressive financing
The objection we hear most: "Everyone thinks we are door-knockers." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How homeowners evaluating solar and battery storage search

Economics-driven: "is solar worth it in [state]", "solar payback period", "solar panel cost [state]".

Questions Solar Installers ask us

How do we escape the door-knocker reputation?

By publishing the math. Honest payback analysis for your specific utility, including cases where solar does not pencil out, is the most trust-building content available in this industry — and it is what people search before they will talk to anyone.

How do we keep up with incentive changes?

A maintained state incentive page with a visible review date. It ranks continuously and it is genuinely useful, which is rare here.

Is battery storage separate?

Increasingly yes, especially in markets with outages or poor net metering. It is a growing, under-served search.
