What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every lead generation decision for agribusinesses.

A conservative industry where relationships and dealer networks dominate
Long purchase cycles synchronized to the crop calendar
Digital marketing is widely assumed not to work on farmers, which is no longer true
The objection we hear most: "Our customers are farmers. They do not research online." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How producers, cooperatives and processors search

Technical and comparative: "best [input] for [crop]", "grain storage cost per bushel", "custom application rates".

Who buys
a marketing or sales lead at an agricultural supplier or processor
Decision cycle
Months, aligned to planting and purchasing seasons.
Deal value
Large contracts with multi-year relationships.
What proves you
Agronomic data, trial results, dealer network reach, and support capability.

What Lead Generation includes

  • Speed-to-lead automation so nothing sits in an inbox
  • Cost-per-signed-job reporting, not cost-per-click

Questions Agribusinesses ask us

Do farmers actually research online?

Overwhelmingly, and they have for a decade. Producers research inputs, equipment and agronomy on their phones from the cab. The assumption that they do not is precisely why this vertical is so cheap to compete in right now.

How do we time content to the crop calendar?

Publish one season ahead. Decisions about seed, inputs and equipment are made months before the purchase, so content that arrives at purchase time arrives too late.

Does this conflict with our dealer network?

It should support it. We build content that generates demand and routes it to dealers, rather than competing with them.
