Questions Med Spas ask us

How do we stop attracting deal shoppers?

Stop leading with price and start leading with the injector. Practices that build content around who is holding the needle, their training and their aesthetic point of view attract patients who choose a person, and those patients stay. Discount-led content attracts discount-led buyers, permanently.

Can we use before-and-after photos?

Yes, with documented consent and no misleading edits. We build the consent workflow alongside the gallery, because an unconsented photo is a real liability.

What is the highest-return content?

Treatment comparison content — Botox versus Dysport, filler versus Sculptra, what a laser actually does. It captures the two weeks of research and it is where practices win the booking.
