What actually gets in the way

Before the tactics, the constraints. These are the three that shape every lead generation decision for accountants & CPA firms.

Extreme seasonality that leaves eight quiet months
Software has commoditized basic tax preparation
Advisory work is more valuable but harder to sell
The objection we hear most: "January through April is the whole year." It is a fair objection, and the program is built to answer it rather than talk around it.

How business owners and individuals with complex returns search

Situation and specialty shaped: "CPA for small business near me", "S corp vs LLC tax", "how to handle [specific tax situation]".

Who buys
a partner at a CPA or accounting firm
Decision cycle
Weeks, concentrated hard around filing deadlines.
Deal value
Recurring annual relationships; advisory work is worth far more than compliance.
What proves you
Specialization by industry, credentials, and clear service tiers.
Seasonality
Filing season dominates, which is exactly why off-season advisory content matters.

Questions Accountants & CPA Firms ask us

How do we sell advisory instead of compliance?

By publishing advisory thinking year-round. Entity structure, owner compensation, quarterly planning and exit preparation are searched in the off-season by the exact clients worth having. Compliance content attracts price shoppers; advisory content attracts relationships.

Should we specialize by industry?

It is the highest-leverage move available. "CPA for [industry]" is specific, searchable and commands better fees than general practice.

When do we publish for tax season?

October and November. Ranking takes time and January is too late.
