Questions Medical Malpractice Lawyers ask us

How do you filter for catastrophic cases?

By building the content around the specific injuries you want — birth injury, anesthesia error, delayed cancer diagnosis — instead of the practice area. Specificity in the content is what produces specificity in the docket.

Cases take years. How do we measure this?

We track signed cases and case type at intake, not settlements. It is the only leading indicator that arrives fast enough to steer by.

Is nursing-home neglect a separate build?

Yes. Different searcher, different emotional register, different decision-maker. It deserves its own cluster.
