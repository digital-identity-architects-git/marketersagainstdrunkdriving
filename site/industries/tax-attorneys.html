Questions Tax Attorneys ask us

How do we look legitimate in a scam-heavy market?

Specificity and restraint. Notice-number content, honest eligibility criteria for an offer in compromise, and no settlement promises. It ranks well precisely because the scam operators cannot write it.

What about the seasonal swing?

Collections and audit content run year-round and smooth the curve. Filing-season traffic is the spike, not the base.

Is this local or national?

Federal tax work is national. State controversy work is local. We usually build both layers.
