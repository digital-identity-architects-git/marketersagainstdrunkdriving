Questions Intellectual Property & Patent Attorneys ask us

How do we compete with filing mills?

By documenting what goes wrong. Office actions, refusals, and the cost of a bad specimen are concrete, searchable, and they make the price difference obvious without you arguing about it.

Is this national or local SEO?

National. IP buyers do not care where you sit, which means no map pack safety net and a much heavier emphasis on content authority.

Who writes the technical content?

It gets drafted against your input and reviewed by your attorneys before publication. In this vertical, technically wrong content costs more credibility than no content.
