Questions Real Estate Attorneys ask us

Our referrals come from agents. Why publish?

Because the content that makes agents look smart to their clients is the content that keeps agents referring. Publishing for the referral source is a different strategy than publishing for the buyer, and it is usually the higher-return one here.

How do we survive a rate-driven slowdown?

By shifting the content mix toward landlord-tenant, title disputes and investor work, which run counter to the purchase cycle.

Is zoning work worth targeting?

Very. Low volume, high value, and almost nobody is publishing seriously about it.
