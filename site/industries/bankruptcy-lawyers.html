Questions Bankruptcy Lawyers ask us

How do you market to people with no money?

By being the firm that says the price out loud. In a market where every competitor hides fees behind a consultation, publishing your flat fee and payment terms is the single highest-converting change available.

Chapter 7 or Chapter 13 first?

Chapter 7 for volume, Chapter 13 for the foreclosure-defense searches, which are more urgent and less price-driven.

Does this work in a strong economy?

Volume drops, but so does competition. Firms that keep publishing through the quiet years own the market when filings spike.
