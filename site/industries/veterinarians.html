Questions Veterinarians ask us

We are at capacity. Why market?

To improve the mix, not the volume. Content built around higher-value services — dentistry, surgery, wellness plans — shifts the schedule toward better economics without adding hours, which is exactly what a capacity-constrained practice needs.

Should we publish prices?

Ranges, yes. It reduces sticker-shock conflict at checkout and it is one of the most-searched things in the category.

What content works best?

Symptom triage — when to worry, when to wait. It builds enormous trust and captures owners at the moment of concern.
