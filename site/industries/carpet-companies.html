Questions Carpet & Soft Flooring Companies ask us

Is carpet still worth marketing?

In bedrooms, basements, multifamily and commercial, yes — and the competition is thinning as everyone chases LVP. There is real, underserved demand for people who still install carpet well and quickly.

Where is the best margin?

Property management and multifamily contracts. That is a B2B content play, not a consumer one, and almost nobody in this trade builds for it.

How do we compete on speed?

Publish your actual lead time. In a category where everyone is vague, a stated turnaround wins jobs outright.
