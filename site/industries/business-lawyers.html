Questions Business & Corporate Attorneys ask us

Is search worth it when volume is this low?

The volume is low and the value per conversion is enormous. Twelve of the right visitors a month can be a better year than a thousand of the wrong ones. We build for that math and report accordingly.

How do we show expertise without breaching confidence?

Write about the pattern, not the client. Structural analysis of a deal type demonstrates judgment far better than a logo wall, and it ranks.

Does LinkedIn matter more than the website here?

They work together. The partner posts, the site converts. We usually run both.
