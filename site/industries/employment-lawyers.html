Questions Employment Lawyers ask us

How do we get fewer unqualified calls?

Qualification belongs in the content. Pages that explain plainly what is and is not actionable in your state filter the pipeline before anyone dials. Fewer calls, better ones — that is the goal we report on.

Can we market to employers and employees at once?

Not on the same site, credibly. Usually we build the plaintiff side as the primary property and give employer-side work its own destination.

What is the highest-value cluster?

Usually wage-and-hour and non-compete. Both have specific, searchable, high-intent questions and both convert.
