Questions Dentists ask us

How do independents compete with DSOs?

On the high-value procedures DSO marketing treats generically. Implants, full-arch, sedation and cosmetic cases have specific, searchable questions and far better economics than fighting over "dentist near me". One implant case can outvalue a month of hygiene traffic.

Can we respond to reviews at all?

Yes, but never confirming someone is a patient. We use a HIPAA-safe response framework that stays warm without disclosing anything.

What is the fastest win?

Google Business Profile plus an accurate "accepting new patients" signal. It moves the needle within weeks.
