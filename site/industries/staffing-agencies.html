Questions Staffing & Recruiting Agencies ask us

How do we serve both audiences?

With two clearly separated tracks from the homepage down. Trying to speak to employers and candidates in the same voice on the same page is the most common and most costly mistake in this industry.

Where is the revenue?

Employers. Candidate content supports supply, but the employer track is what should be optimized for conversion.

How do we specialize?

By industry and role type. "Manufacturing staffing agency [region]" beats "staffing agency" on every metric that matters.
