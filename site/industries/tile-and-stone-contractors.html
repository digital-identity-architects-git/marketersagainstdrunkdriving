Questions Tile & Stone Contractors ask us

How do we get paid for craft?

By making the invisible visible. Content on waterproofing systems, substrate prep and why showers fail gives homeowners a reason to care about who installs, and it is what people search after a leak.

Should we target remodelers or homeowners?

Both, on separate pages. Trade referral content and homeowner content need different language entirely.

What is the best-converting page?

Usually the shower remodel cost page. High intent, high value, and it maps directly to how people search.
