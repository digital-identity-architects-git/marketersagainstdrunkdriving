Questions Epoxy & Concrete Coating Companies ask us

How do we compete with the franchises?

On chemistry and failure modes. Content explaining why cheap epoxy peels, what moisture testing does, and how polyaspartic differs positions you as the expert. Franchises sell a brand; you can sell knowing what you are doing, and homeowners searching "epoxy floor peeling" are already primed for it.

Is commercial worth separate pages?

Very much. Higher ticket, less competition, and completely different search language.

What kills the DIY objection?

Honest content about surface prep. Once a homeowner understands grinding versus etching, the kit stops looking like the same product.
