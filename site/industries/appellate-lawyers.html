Questions Appellate Attorneys ask us

Should we market to lawyers or to clients?

To lawyers, primarily. The content that wins here is written for trial counsel — preservation of error, standard of review, when to bring in appellate counsel pre-verdict. That audience is small, findable, and worth a great deal.

Does that reassure or threaten the referring firm?

It reassures, if the framing is co-counsel rather than replacement. That framing is a deliberate editorial choice on every page.

Is the volume really there?

No, and it does not need to be. This is a practice where a dozen right readers a month is a business.
