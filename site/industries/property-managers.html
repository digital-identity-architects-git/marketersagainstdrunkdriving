Questions Property Management Companies ask us

How do we sell against self-management?

With the cost of getting it wrong. Eviction timelines, security deposit law, fair housing exposure and vacancy cost are all concrete, searchable and terrifying to an owner who has just had a bad tenant. That content converts far better than a fee comparison.

Should we publish our fees?

Yes. Owners are comparing regardless, and transparency wins the shortlist.

What is the best acquisition content?

State-specific landlord law. It ranks well, it is genuinely useful, and it reaches owners at the exact moment self-management stops feeling free.
