Questions Insurance Agencies ask us

How do we compete with direct carriers?

By not competing on personal auto. Commercial lines by trade — "insurance for roofing contractors", "insurance for med spas" — is specific, searched, high-margin and completely ignored by the national advertisers.

Which commercial niches work best?

The ones with unusual risk that carriers handle inconsistently. Contractors, trucking, hospitality and professional liability all qualify.

Does content help retention?

Yes. Renewal-season email explaining coverage changes measurably reduces shopping.
