Design-build work has the margin but a much longer sell

Questions Landscaping Companies ask us

How do we get out of price competition on maintenance?

Use maintenance as the recurring base and market hardscape and design-build as the profit centre. Patio, outdoor kitchen and retaining wall content attracts a completely different customer than "lawn mowing near me" — one who is not comparing you to a truck and a mower.

Spring is everything. When do we start?

January. The content and profile work has to be ranked before the demand arrives, not started when the phone should already be ringing.

Is commercial worth pursuing?

Yes — recurring contracts, less seasonality, and far less marketing competition.
