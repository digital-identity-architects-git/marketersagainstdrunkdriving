Same-day to a few days. Largely impulse and occasion-driven.

What proves you

Questions Day Spas ask us

How do we compete with the franchises?

On the experience and the therapists, which the franchise model structurally cannot sell. Local SEO and photography of your actual room beat a national brand template in the map pack more often than owners expect.

Where does the fastest return come from?

Google Business Profile, without much competition for the honour. Weekly posts, current photos, and rapid review response move day-spa bookings faster than anything else available.

How do we fill weekday gaps?

Segmented email and SMS to your existing list with same-week availability. Your list is the cheapest inventory-filling tool you own.
