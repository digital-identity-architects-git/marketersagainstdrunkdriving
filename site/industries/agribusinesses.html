Technical and comparative: "best [input] for [crop]", "grain storage cost per bushel", "custom application rates".

Questions Agribusinesses ask us

Do farmers actually research online?

Overwhelmingly, and they have for a decade. Producers research inputs, equipment and agronomy on their phones from the cab. The assumption that they do not is precisely why this vertical is so cheap to compete in right now.

How do we time content to the crop calendar?

Publish one season ahead. Decisions about seed, inputs and equipment are made months before the purchase, so content that arrives at purchase time arrives too late.

Does this conflict with our dealer network?

It should support it. We build content that generates demand and routes it to dealers, rather than competing with them.
