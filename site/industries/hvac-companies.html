Questions HVAC Companies ask us

How do independents compete with roll-ups?

On the symptom searches the roll-ups ignore. "AC blowing warm air" and its hundred siblings have huge volume, low competition and immediate intent — while the roll-ups spend their money fighting over "HVAC repair near me". Owning the symptom layer is both cheaper and higher-converting.

How do we smooth the seasonality?

Maintenance agreements, marketed in the shoulder months with content about pre-season tune-ups and indoor air quality. That is the difference between a lumpy year and a stable one.

Does the map pack still decide it?

For emergency work, largely yes. Profile management is not optional in this trade.
