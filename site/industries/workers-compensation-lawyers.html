Questions Workers Compensation Lawyers ask us

How does the math work under fee caps?

It works on cost per signed case, which is why we build around the denial and retaliation searches — high intent, lower competition than the head term, and they convert at a rate that survives the cap.

Do we need Spanish content?

In most industrial markets, yes, and it is usually the single biggest untapped channel in this practice area.

Is comp worth separating from personal injury?

Yes. Shared sites confuse both audiences and dilute the topical signal for each.
