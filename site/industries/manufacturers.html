Questions Manufacturers ask us

Do engineers really search for suppliers?

Constantly, and by capability rather than company name. A page specifying materials, tolerances, certifications and lot sizes will be found by procurement teams that have never heard of you. Most manufacturer sites publish none of that.

What should the site actually contain?

Your equipment list, your certifications, your tolerances and your industries served. Specificity is the entire strategy in this vertical.

Is this worth it against trade show spend?

It typically costs a fraction of one show and works every day of the year.
