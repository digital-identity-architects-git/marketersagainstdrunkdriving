Questions B2B SaaS Companies ask us

Our category is saturated. Where do we win?

In the segment and the alternative. "[Category] software for [specific vertical]" and "[competitor] alternative" have real intent, far less competition, and they convert far better than the head term you cannot rank for anyway.

Does content still work post-AI-flood?

Content with proprietary data, real product screenshots and actual customer numbers works better than it did, because the generic middle has become worthless. The bar moved up, which favours anyone willing to clear it.

How do we attribute a committee sale?

Self-reported attribution at the form plus first-touch tracking. Neither alone is sufficient; together they are usable.
